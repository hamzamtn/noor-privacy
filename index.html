Privacy Policy: Noor Companion

App: Noor Companion (Android package com.noorcompanion.islamic_daily)

Effective date: 24 November 2026 (replaces the version of 23 November 2026)

Contact: srwebgeek@gmail.com

Summary

  • Noor Companion does not collect, store on any server, sell or share your personal data. We have no servers and no user accounts.
  • The app has no ads, no analytics, no crash-reporting and no tracking SDKs.
  • Your location is used only on your phone to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Everything you create (settings, Salah and fasting journal, Qaza backlog, Tasbeeh counts, bookmarks, Khatm plan, home tiles) stays on your device.
  • The app connects to the internet only when you tap to do so: to download or stream (play online) Quran recitation or translation audio from everyayah.com, or to check for or download a tafsir update from quranenc.com when you tap Check for update or Download for a tafsir edition. It never connects on its own, and it sends none of your personal data.
  • Report a correction only opens your own email app with a pre-filled message. The app itself sends nothing.
  • Nearby masjid only opens your own maps app (for example Google Maps) to search for mosques near you. The app itself sends nothing; the maps app handles the search under its own privacy policy.

1. Who we are

Noor Companion ("the app", "we") is a free Islamic daily companion app: prayer times, Adhan alerts, Qibla, Quran with tafsir, hadith, Masnoon duas, stories of the prophets from the Quran and hadith, Tasbeeh, azkar, a Khatm (Quran-reading) planner and a personal Salah journal. You can reach the developer at srwebgeek@gmail.com.

4. Optional internet connections (everyayah.com and quranenc.com)

The app uses the internet in only two situations, and only after you tap a button. It makes no network request when it starts, in the background or on a schedule.

4.1 Recitation and translation audio (everyayah.com)

Quran recitation and human-voiced translation audio are not bundled with the app because of their size. When you tap to download a surah, the app downloads the audio files over a secure (HTTPS) connection from everyayah.com, a third-party public audio library. The files are then stored on your device and played offline. If you tap Play online instead, the app streams the same files from the same server over HTTPS while you listen: nothing is saved on your device, and before it starts the app makes one small request for the first file to check the connection. If you choose Always play online in Settings › Audio, tapping Listen on a surah that isn't downloaded streams it in the same way.

4.2 Tafsir updates (quranenc.com)

Two Arabic tafsir editions (Al-Mukhtasar in Interpreting the Noble Quran, and At-Tafsir Al-Muyassar) are included in the app and work offline. Their text comes from QuranEnc.com (Encyclopedia of the Noble Quran) and is shown unchanged.

  • The app never checks for tafsir updates by itself.
  • When you tap Check for update (Settings › Appearance › Tafsir › Updates & downloads), the app asks quranenc.com over HTTPS for the current version number of that edition.
  • When you tap Download, the app downloads that edition's text from quranenc.com over HTTPS (one file per surah, about 9–11 MB per edition). It asks for the version number again before and after, so that all files belong to the same version. The text is then stored on your device and read offline. Remove download deletes it, and the app goes back to the copy included in the app.

4.3 What these websites receive

  • Like any web server, everyayah.com (whether you download or stream (play online) the audio) and quranenc.com receive the standard information that comes with every internet request: your device's IP address, the file being requested (which reciter and surah, or which tafsir edition and surah) and basic technical request headers. That information is handled under each website's own practices, which we do not control.
  • The app does not send your name, location, any account details, device identifiers or any of your journal or settings data with these requests.
  • If you never download or stream (play online) audio and never tap Check for update or Download for a tafsir, the app never connects to the internet.

5. Features that use your phone's own services

  • Read-aloud: translations are read using your phone's built-in text-to-speech engine (for example, Google Speech Services). The app passes the text to that engine on your device. How that engine works, including whether it uses voices online, is set by its provider and your phone's settings.
  • Nearby masjid: tapping Nearby masjid opens your own maps app (Google Maps, Apple Maps or another maps app) with a search for mosques. On Android the app passes your saved prayer-times location to the maps app on your phone so the search starts in the right area; if no maps app is installed, Google Maps opens in your browser without any location from the app. The app itself sends nothing to any server. The search, the map and any directions are handled by the maps app under its own privacy policy.
  • Report a correction: tapping Report a correction (in Settings, the Disclaimer, the User guide, and next to Quran, hadith, dua, dhikr and tafsir texts) opens your own email app through a standard mailto: link. The message is addressed to srwebgeek@gmail.com and pre-filled with the app version, the app language and the item reference (for example a surah and ayah, or a hadith number), with blank lines for you to describe the mistake. The app itself sends nothing. The email is sent only if you press Send in your email app; it then goes through your email provider to the developer, who uses it only to check and fix the reported text. Corrections about tafsir text are forwarded to QuranEnc.com, as stated in the Tafsir sheet. If no email app is found, the app shows the address for you to copy and lets you share the report text through your phone's share sheet.
  • Sharing, PDF export and backups: when you share a verse image or timetable, export a PDF, or export a backup, the app opens your phone's share sheet, print dialog or file picker. You choose where the file goes (for example a messaging app or cloud drive). Once you send it, that service's own privacy policy applies.
  • Backup file: the backup is a plain (unencrypted) file with your settings and journal. It is created only when you ask for it, and only you decide where it goes. Keep it somewhere private. Restoring reads a file you pick; nothing is downloaded.
  • Android system backup: if Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include this app's on-device data in your personal device backup. That backup is managed by Google under your account settings, not by us, and we cannot access it.

6. What we do not do

  • We do not collect personal data, and we do not have a server that receives it.
  • We do not sell, rent, trade or share any data with anyone.
  • We do not show ads or use advertising IDs.
  • We do not use analytics, crash-reporting, profiling or tracking tools.
  • We do not require an account, sign-in, email address or phone number.

7. Children

The app is suitable for general audiences, but it is not directed at children under 13. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children. A parent or guardian with any question can contact us at the address below.

8. Keeping and deleting your data

All of your data lives only on your device, so you control it fully:

  • Delete downloaded audio: Settings › Audio › Downloaded recitations › delete.
  • Delete a downloaded tafsir update: Settings › Appearance › Tafsir › Remove download.
  • Delete everything: Android Settings › Apps › Noor Companion › Storage › Clear storage (or Clear data).
  • Uninstall the app: this removes all of its data from your device.
  • Backup files you exported are separate files. Delete them wherever you saved them.

Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on a server. You do not need to send us a deletion request.

9. Security

Your data is kept in the app's private storage, which Android protects from other apps. Audio downloads and streaming (play online), and tafsir downloads, use HTTPS. Please protect your phone with a screen lock and keep any exported backup file private.

10. Changes to this policy

If the app's handling of information changes (for example, a new feature that needs the internet), we will update this policy and change the effective date above before that version is released. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
